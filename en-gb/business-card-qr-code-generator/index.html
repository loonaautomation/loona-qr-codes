About this Business Card

Ninety percent of paper business cards are thrown away within a week — the ones that survive are the ones that became a phone contact at the moment of the handshake. A vCard QR code on the back of the card does that job: scan, tap Save, and you are in their phone with name, title, company, number, email and website.

Design rules that matter: keep the front clean, put the code on the back at 2 cm or larger, hold the quiet zone sacred even when the designer wants a border there, and test the physical card — glossy finishes reflect scanner light, so matte stock scans best.

Best uses

  • Networking and conferences
  • Sales and site-visit cards
  • Freelancer and trades cards
  • Realtor and consultant cards

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

The code stores a standard vCard (VERSION 3.0) — the same format phones have saved for twenty years — generated locally in your browser. Update your number? Reprint: the contact lives in the pattern, so generate the card after any detail changes.

Worked example

A consultant prints 200 cards with the vCard code on the back. At a two-day expo, 60+ scans land her directly in phones; the follow-up emails start with “saved your number at the expo” instead of silence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Loona’s Business Card generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Business Card page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

vCard code or link to my website?

vCard for the card itself — the goal at a handshake is to be saved as a contact. The vCard can carry your website inside it, so you lose nothing.

What size should the code be on a card?

At least 20 × 20 mm, on the back, with white margin around it. vCards hold more data than a URL, so the pattern is denser — do not shrink it.
