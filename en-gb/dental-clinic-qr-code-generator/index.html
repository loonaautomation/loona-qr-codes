About this Dental & Clinic

Clinics hand out three kinds of paper that QR codes replace well: appointment cards (book the next visit from the chair), aftercare leaflets (whitening, extraction and brace-care instructions as a phone-friendly page), and intake forms (filled on the patient’s own phone in the waiting room).

Never put personal health information inside a QR code — the pattern is readable by any camera. Codes should carry links and public instructions only, which is also how this generator is designed: it encodes the link you give it, nothing medical.

Best uses

  • Appointment booking at reception
  • Aftercare instruction cards
  • Patient intake forms
  • Check-in confirmation

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

A static URL QR to your booking system, form or aftercare page. Because generation happens in the browser, no patient data passes through Loona at any point.

Worked example

An orthodontic practice prints brace-care codes on a wallet card. Emergency calls about broken brackets drop — patients scan, watch the 2-minute care video, and book only if still unsure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Dental & Clinic generator free?

Yes. Loona’s Dental & Clinic generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Dental & Clinic page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Is it safe to use QR codes with patients?

Yes, for links to public pages and forms. Never encode names, record numbers or treatment details in the pattern itself — anyone can read a QR code.

What about patients without smartphones?

Keep the paper route alongside. QR is an addition that cuts queues for most patients, not a replacement that excludes anyone.
