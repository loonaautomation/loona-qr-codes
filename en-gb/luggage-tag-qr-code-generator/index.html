About this Luggage Tag

Airline tags get torn, soaked and separated. A QR luggage tag — printed on a tough card, a sticker inside the lid AND one in the front pocket — gives any finder or agent your contact details from a phone scan: name, mobile, email, home city. Redundancy is the strategy: three cheap tags beat one beautiful one.

Include a number that works internationally (full country code) and an email you actually read while travelling. Do not print your full home address on an outside tag — contact details are enough for reunions.

Best uses

  • Checked suitcases and backpacks
  • Instrument and equipment cases
  • School and sports kit bags
  • Conference and exhibition freight

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

The code encodes your contact text directly as a plain-text QR — no account, no tracking, readable offline by any camera.

Worked example

A delayed bag reaches the wrong airport; the agent scans the QR tag inside the lid, calls the mobile number, and the bag is re-routed the same evening instead of sitting in a lost-property cage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Luggage Tag generator free?

Yes. Loona’s Luggage Tag generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Luggage Tag page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Where should I put the tags?

One outside on the handle, one inside the lid, one in a pocket. Bags are found by whoever opens them — make sure every opening finds a tag.

Should the tag include my home address?

No. A phone number and email reunite bags; a full address on an external tag advertises an empty house.
