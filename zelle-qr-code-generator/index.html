About this Zelle

Zelle has no universal payment-link standard like UPI or SEPA — payments are sent inside each bank’s app to an enrolled email or US mobile number, and typing that detail wrong is the main failure. This generator makes the next best thing: a neat information-card QR that displays your exact enrolled name and email/number when scanned, ready to copy into the banking app without a transcription error.

Be precise: the name must match how you enrolled (banks show it to the sender for confirmation), and double-check the email/number digit by digit. Zelle payments are fast and hard to reverse, so the payer should always confirm the displayed name before sending.

Best uses

  • Freelancer and contractor payments
  • Rent and roommate splits
  • Local services
  • Family and community collections

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

The code encodes a plain-text payment card (method, name, send-to, note) that any camera displays. It deliberately does NOT pretend to be a deep link — no such cross-bank standard exists for Zelle, and honesty here protects your payers.

Worked example

A piano teacher prints the card for her studio: parents scan, see “Zelle — J. Alvarez — jalvarez@example.com”, and pay from their own banking app with the details copied exactly.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Zelle page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Why doesn’t the code open the payer’s bank app?

Because Zelle lives inside 2,000+ different banking apps with no shared link format. Some banks offer their own in-app QR receive codes; this card works with every bank because it carries the details themselves.

Is it safe to print my Zelle email?

It is the same information you would read out or text to a payer. Share the card with people you are billing, as you would an invoice.
