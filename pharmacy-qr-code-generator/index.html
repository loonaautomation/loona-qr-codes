About this Pharmacy

Pharmacies repeat the same three conversations all day: how to request a repeat prescription, where to find reliable information about a medicine, and how to book services like flu vaccination and blood-pressure checks. A counter code and a bag-sticker code answer all three on the patient’s own phone, in the queue or at home.

Health links must land on trustworthy pages — your pharmacy’s own service pages or official patient-information leaflets. Never encode personal prescription details in a code; the pattern is readable by anyone, and this generator only ever encodes the link you give it.

Best uses

  • Repeat-prescription requests
  • Medicine information leaflets
  • Vaccination and health-check booking
  • Home-delivery service signup

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

A static URL QR to your pharmacy’s page. Because generation is local to your browser, no patient information passes through Loona at all.

Worked example

A community pharmacy adds a refill code to its bag stickers. Refill requests move from phone calls at the busiest hour to a quiet online queue the team clears between rushes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Pharmacy generator free?

Yes. Loona’s Pharmacy generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Pharmacy page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Can the code contain dosage instructions for a patient?

No — never put personal medical details in a QR pattern. Link to the general information leaflet; patient-specific instructions belong in the dispensing process.

Bag sticker size?

At least 2.5 cm, high contrast, on a flat part of the bag or a card inside it — and test-scan a finished bag before ordering a roll of stickers.
