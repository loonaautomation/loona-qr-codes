About this Feedback Form

Feedback quality decays by the hour — the form emailed tomorrow gets the polite version; the code scanned at the table gets the truth. Restaurants, clinics, trainers and shops place feedback codes where the experience just ended: on receipts, exit doors, packaging and certificates.

Short forms win: five questions maximum, one of them free text. And close the loop publicly — a “you said, we changed” note on the wall beside the code doubles response rates.

Best uses

  • Receipts and invoices
  • Tables, counters and exit doors
  • Training and workshop handouts
  • Product packaging inserts

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

The code carries your form link (Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Typeform…) as a static URL QR. Edit or replace the form behind your link service as your questions evolve.

Worked example

A training centre prints the code on its certificates. Feedback moves from 6 polite emails per course to 40 specific responses — including the broken-projector complaint it fixes the same week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Feedback Form generator free?

Yes. Loona’s Feedback Form generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Feedback Form page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Feedback form or Google review code?

Both, for different jobs: the form gives you private, fixable detail; the review code builds public reputation. Many businesses run the form first, then invite happy respondents to review.

How many questions should the form have?

Five or fewer. Every extra question costs more responses than the answer is worth.
