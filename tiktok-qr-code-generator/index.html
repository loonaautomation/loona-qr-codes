Where to find your TikTok username and profile link

You do not need to guess your link. Your TikTok profile address is always https://www.tiktok.com/@yourusername. To confirm your exact username, open the TikTok app, tap Profile at the bottom, and look at the name under your profile picture — that is your username. To copy the full link instead, tap Share profile (or the share arrow on your profile) and choose Copy link, then paste that link into the form above; the tool accepts the full link as well as the plain username, with or without the @.

TikTok usernames are 2 to 24 characters and can contain letters, numbers, underscores and periods, but cannot end in a period. TikTok only lets you change your username once every 30 days, and changing it changes your profile link — so if you rename your account, generate a fresh QR code and replace your old prints, because the old code still points to the old address.

TikTok has a built-in profile code inside the app (open your profile and look for the QR code option near your username / in the menu). That code is designed mainly to be scanned from inside TikTok by people who already use the app. The code you make here is a standard QR code: any phone camera or scanner app can read it, it opens your profile in the TikTok app when installed and in the browser when it is not, and you choose its colour, background and download size for print. For posters, cards, menus and packaging — where you cannot assume the scanner has TikTok open — a standard QR code is the safer choice.

How to scan a TikTok QR code

Most people never need a special app: point the ordinary phone camera at the code and tap the link that appears. Inside TikTok, you can also tap the search icon at the top and then the scan icon in the search bar to use TikTok's own scanner. Always test your finished code both ways — with your camera and, if you can, with a second phone — before you print it in quantity.

Download and print guidance

Choose the size by where the code will live: 256 px is fine for a screen or a small digital graphic, 512 px suits business cards, receipts and packaging, and 1024 px is the right choice for posters, banners and anything printed large. Print the code at least 2 cm wide (about 1 inch for close-up scanning, larger for distance), keep the white border — the quiet zone — around it, and avoid printing on shiny, curved or textured surfaces where reflections break up the pattern. Download the PNG, place it in your card, poster or menu design without stretching it, test-scan the final printed proof once, and only then print the full run. You can also share the PNG digitally — in a story, a link-in-bio page or another platform's post — so followers there can scan or long-press to reach your TikTok profile.

Static code: what that means for editing and tracking

Loona codes are static: your profile link is written directly into the pattern. That is why the code never expires, has no scan limit and works without any account. The honest trade-offs are that a static code cannot be edited after printing, cannot be redirected to a new username or a single video later, and does not count or track scans — this page offers no scan analytics. If tracking or changing the destination after printing matters more to you than a code that can never be switched off, you would need a dynamic QR service; for a permanent profile code on print, static is the dependable option.
