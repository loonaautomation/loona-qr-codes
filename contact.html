Contact Loona QR Codes

Questions, corrections or a QR type you wish Loona had? We would like to hear from you.

Because Loona is a small free project by Loona Automation, the fastest contact route is GitHub: open an Issue on the Loona QR Codes GitHub repository with your question or suggestion, and include the name of the generator page you were using. You can also see other Loona projects on the Loona Automation GitHub profile.

Prefer email? You can also write to us directly at loonaautomation@gmail.com — please mention the name of the generator page you were using.

Please never send passwords, private keys, seed phrases or sensitive personal documents when contacting us — Loona never needs them, for support or for generating any QR code.

Loona QR Codes — Free QR Code Generator — No Expiry, No Signup, No Watermark. Every QR code is generated privately in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server, codes never expire and there is no watermark.
