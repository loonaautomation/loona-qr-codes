About this Product Packaging

Packaging space is rationed, but a QR code turns the pack into a doorway: full instructions in twelve languages, the brand story, a warranty registration, an authenticity check, or a one-tap reorder. Food and cosmetics brands also use on-pack codes for the ingredient and recycling detail that regulations increasingly require but labels cannot fit.

Packaging print is unforgiving: demand a vector-sharp source (use the 1024 px download), keep the quiet zone empty even when the design team wants the code “integrated”, and proof-scan from the actual printed pack, in shop lighting, before the run of 50,000.

Best uses

  • Instruction manuals and setup videos
  • Warranty registration
  • Authenticity verification
  • Ingredients, recycling and compliance detail

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

A static URL QR to the product page you control. Batch and serial data belong in the page address pattern your system uses (for example one code per SKU, resolving serials on the page).

Worked example

A cookware brand prints one code on the box: scan → care guide, warranty registration and a reorder link for spare lids. Support emails asking “how do I season this?” fall away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Product Packaging generator free?

Yes. Loona’s Product Packaging generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this Product Packaging page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

One code for all units, or one per unit?

One per SKU for information pages. Per-unit unique codes are for anti-counterfeit and traceability systems, which generate codes in bulk from your serial database — see the Bulk QR Code Generator.

Can the code survive being printed small on a curved jar?

Keep it at least 15 mm, on the flattest area, and test on the real container. High-contrast black on white remains the most forgiving combination.
