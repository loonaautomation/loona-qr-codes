Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Loona QR Codes is designed to collect as little as technically possible: nothing.

All QR code generation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. The text, passwords, phone numbers, payment details and links you enter are never sent to, processed by, or stored on any Loona server — Loona has no server and no user accounts.

This static website may be hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own privacy policy. If advertising (such as Google AdSense) is added in future, advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads; you can control cookies in your browser settings. This policy will be updated before any such change goes live.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information because we do not receive any. Questions: see the Contact page.
