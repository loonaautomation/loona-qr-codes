About this QR Code with Logo

A logo in the center is the one customization businesses actually want from a QR code — and the one most likely to break it when done carelessly. It works because QR codes are built with spare capacity: at error-correction level H, roughly 30% of the pattern can be missing or covered and the code still reads. The logo spends part of that budget — which is why size discipline and test-scanning are non-negotiable.

This generator composes everything on a 1024 px canvas in your browser: the code at level H, a white padded plate behind your logo (logos placed directly on the pattern scan noticeably worse), and your logo centered at the size you choose. Nothing is uploaded; your logo never leaves your device.

Best uses

  • Branded menus and packaging
  • Business cards and signage
  • Event and conference material
  • Product labels and stickers

Put the code exactly where the decision is made — on the table, the counter, the packaging, the invitation or the screen — and give people one short line of instruction such as “Scan to open” or “Scan to pay”. Print codes at least 2 cm wide with a quiet white border and strong contrast so older phone cameras read them quickly; for posters scanned from a distance, use the 1024 px size and print bigger — a good guide is roughly one tenth of the scanning distance.

How it works

Start conservative: the “small” logo setting (18%) scans almost everywhere. Medium (22%) suits simple, bold logos. Large (26%) spends most of the safe budget — use it only after scanning the result with several phones, including an older Android, in the light where it will live. Keep the logo’s own contrast strong, and never let it cover the three corner squares of the pattern.

Worked example

A café adds its round badge logo at the medium setting to its menu code. The badge sits on its white plate, the code scans on every phone at the table, and the menu finally looks like part of the brand instead of a stamp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR Code with Logo generator free?

Yes. Loona’s QR Code with Logo generator is completely free with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many codes you create, for personal or commercial use.

Will my QR code expire?

No. Loona creates static QR codes: your information is encoded directly inside the QR pattern itself, not stored on a server or hidden behind a paid redirect. A code you print today will keep working as long as the destination itself stays active.

Is my information private on this QR Code with Logo page?

Yes. Generation happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to or stored on any server.

Why does the logo need a white plate behind it?

The plate guarantees contrast between the logo and the pattern modules and keeps the modules under the logo area uniformly covered, which scanners handle far better than a busy logo edge directly on the pattern.

My logo code stopped scanning at the large size. What now?

Drop one size, simplify the logo (a badge or icon version scans better than a detailed lockup), and re-test. If it still fails, the code’s content is too dense — shorten the link first.
