About Loona QR Codes

Loona QR Codes is a free online QR code generator built to solve one simple problem honestly: making QR codes that never expire, without signup, watermarks or paid traps.

Loona offers 30 focused generators — for websites, WiFi, WhatsApp, contact cards, social profiles, locations, reviews, menus, events and payments including PayPal, UPI and, for Pakistan, JazzCash/EasyPaisa information QR codes. Every generator runs entirely in your browser, so your data is never uploaded to a server.

Loona is free for personal and commercial use. If Loona saved you time, the best support is sharing it with a friend, a shop or a class that needs a QR code.
