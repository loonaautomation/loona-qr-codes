Feedback & Bug Reports

Did a QR code fail to scan, a download go wrong, or a generator behave strangely? Please tell us — reports from visitors are how this free site gets fixed and improved.

How to reach us

Email us at loonaautomation@gmail.com. There is no form on this page on purpose: a plain email reaches us directly, and you keep a copy of what you sent.

✉ Email your feedback

What to include

· The generator you used (for example, "WiFi QR Code").
· What happened — and what you expected instead.
· Whether the code scanned or not, and on which phone if you know it.
· Your device and browser (for example, "Android phone, Chrome").

What happens next

We read every message, reproduce the problem where we can, fix it, and test the generator again — including a real scan test — before the fix goes live. Please never send passwords, private keys or sensitive personal documents: Loona never needs them, for support or for generating any QR code.

Loona QR Codes — Free QR Code Generator — No Expiry, No Signup, No Watermark. Every QR code is generated privately in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server, codes never expire and there is no watermark.
